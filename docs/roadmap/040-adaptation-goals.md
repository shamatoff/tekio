# Roadmap: Adaptation goals — declaring what I am chasing this cycle

**Label:** backlog
**Status:** backlog — parked on purpose. The Adaptations read is now rebuilt
([031](done/031-adaptations-drill-down-read.md), 2026-09-04) and grounded
([039](done/039-adaptations-read-grounding.md)); revisit once the page has been lived
with long enough to say whether a flat seven-way read feels wrong in practice.
**Depends:** 031, 039

## The idea

Today every adaptation is chased equally, all the time. The weekly target is the
same whatever I am actually training for, so a strength block reads as a
hypertrophy failure and a base-building month reads as an anaerobic gap.

A **goal** would say what I am chasing *this cycle* — prioritise strength, hold
hypertrophy, ignore anaerobic capacity for six weeks — and "what's missing"
would be measured against that intent instead of against a flat seven.

## Why it is parked, not planned

Three reasons, and none of them is "it is hard":

1. **The read has to be right first.** A goal is a filter over an answer. Until
   031 has rebuilt the answer and 039 has decided what its numbers mean, a goal
   filters something that is still moving.
2. **It has to feel necessary, not clever.** The doctrine's exit condition is
   the five-second Home read. If the flat seven-way read turns out to be enough
   in daily use, this feature is decoration and should never be built. That is a
   judgement only made *after* living with the rebuilt page — which is what the
   status line is waiting for.
3. **It sits very close to P4.** *"Configurability is not a decision."* The
   defence is that a goal is not a preference — it is a **training input**, the
   same kind of thing as a program cycle, and it changes what the app *says*
   rather than what it *shows*. That defence has to be made properly at kickoff,
   not assumed. If it collapses, the honest answer is that the program already
   encodes intent and the goal is a second copy of it.

## What it is not

- **Not the weekly-target editor.** Admin already edits `weeklyMuscleTarget` /
  `weeklySessionTarget`. Those say *what adequate looks like* — a physiological
  claim, grounded in [011](done/011-adaptation-weekly-targets.md). A goal says
  *what I am chasing*, which is intent and needs no grounding at all. Do not
  build the second by widening the first.
- **Not [012](012-adaptation-target-shapes.md).** That fixes the *unit* a target
  is expressed in. Orthogonal.
- **Not a new section** (R1). It would be a modifier on the Adaptations read and
  on Home's verdict, not a destination (P3).

## Questions to answer before it becomes `planned`

- Does the six-week cycle already carry this? A program *is* a declaration of
  intent; a goal may be a duplicate of something the program should own.
- Is a goal per adaptation (prioritise / hold / ignore) or one focus per cycle?
- Does a goal change the *target*, the *ranking* of gaps, or only Home's
  sentence? Only the last of those leaves the numbers grounded.
- What happens at cycle rollover — does the goal expire with the cycle?
